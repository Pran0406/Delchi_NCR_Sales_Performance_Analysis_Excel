# 📊 Delhi NCR Sales Performance Analysis — Excel

An end-to-end **📈 sales performance analysis project using Microsoft Excel**, focused on analyzing e-commerce sales across Delhi NCR. The project covers data cleaning, sales and profit analysis, regional and category performance, customer analysis, product performance, order status, and monthly sales trends.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful **💡 business insights** that can support **sales monitoring, regional strategy, product planning, and data-driven decision-making**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **30,000 e-commerce orders** across **9 regions** and **8 product categories**.

The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel**, with a focus on:

* 🧹 Data cleaning and validation
* 💰 Sales and profit analysis
* 🌍 Regional performance
* 🏷️ Category performance
* 🛍️ Product performance
* 👥 Customer analysis
* 📦 Order status analysis
* 📅 Monthly sales trends
* 🎯 KPI reporting
* 📊 Interactive dashboard visualization

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Before analysis, the dataset was reviewed and cleaned to improve data quality.

### 🔧 Cleaning activities included:

* 🔍 Identifying missing values
* ⚠️ Checking inconsistent values
* 🧹 Handling missing rating and delivery information for cancelled/returned orders
* 🔢 Correcting invalid discount values
* 📊 Reviewing numerical fields for anomalies
* ✅ Validating sales and profit-related calculations
* ⚙️ Preparing the dataset for PivotTable-based analysis

The cleaned data was then used to build analytical summaries and dashboard visualizations.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The Excel dashboard provides multiple views of sales performance.

### 1️⃣ Order Distribution

Analyzes the distribution of orders across:

* ✅ Delivered
* ❌ Cancelled
* 🔄 Returned

### 2️⃣ Sales by Category

Compares sales contribution across the different product categories.

### 3️⃣ Segment / Status Contribution

Shows the percentage contribution of different order segments/statuses.

### 4️⃣ Top 5 Customers

Compares the **Top 5 customers based on sales and profit performance**.

### 5️⃣ Regional Sales Performance 🌍

Analyzes sales performance across the 9 regions to identify stronger and weaker markets.

### 6️⃣ Top 5 Products 🏆

Identifies the products contributing the highest sales.

### 7️⃣ Monthly Sales Trend 📅

Tracks sales performance over time and helps identify changes in demand and seasonal patterns.

### 8️⃣ Sales vs Profit Analysis 💰

Compares revenue generation with profitability to identify categories/products that generate strong sales but may have comparatively lower profit.

## 💡 Key Insights

### 📦 Order Status

* ✅ Delivered orders account for approximately **66.80%** of total orders.
* ❌ Cancelled orders represent approximately **16.76%**.
* 🔄 Returned orders represent approximately **16.44%**.
* ⚠️ Cancellation and return rates together indicate an important opportunity for improving order fulfillment and customer experience.

### 💰 Sales Performance

* 💵 Total analyzed sales are approximately **₹47.34 Crore**.
* 🏷️ Category-level analysis helps identify major revenue contributors.
* 📊 Comparing sales with profit provides a better view of business performance than sales alone.

### 🌍 Regional Performance

Regional analysis helps identify:

* 🟢 High-performing markets
* 🔴 Lower-performing regions
* 📊 Regional sales concentration
* 🚀 Opportunities for improving underperforming markets

### 🛍️ Product Performance

Top-product analysis helps identify products responsible for a significant share of sales and provides useful information for **inventory and sales planning**.

### 📅 Monthly Trends

Monthly sales analysis helps identify:

* 🔥 High-demand periods
* 📉 Low-performing months
* 🔄 Seasonal patterns
* 🎯 Potential opportunities for promotional planning
